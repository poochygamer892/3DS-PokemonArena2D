# Poke2D Arena web assets

The `source/` images in this folder were downloaded from the public game at
[poke2darena.com/play-online](https://poke2darena.com/play-online/).

- Arena backgrounds: `play-online/stages/{volcanic_rect,grass_rect}/arena.png`
- Directional Pokémon sheets: `assets/movement/<Pokédex ID>/{Idle,Walk,Shoot}-Anim.png`
- Animation frame sizes and pivots: `movementClips.generated-CxVZ_dLI.js`

These are the resources served by the reference game, included here so the
personal 3DS port can be built offline. The upstream game's asset permissions
and trademarks remain with their respective owners. The 3DS port is unofficial
and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the upstream game's developers or
Nintendo.
